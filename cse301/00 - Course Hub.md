# CSE 301 — Mathematics for Computing and Data Science

> 3 hours/week · 3 credits

## Course Description

Advanced counting and discrete probability, random variables and distributions,
probability bounds, convergence, statistical inference, Bayesian inference,
hypothesis testing, stochastic processes, and queuing theory.

---

# Syllabus

## 1. Counting and Discrete Probability

- Recurrences and Sums
- Binomial Coefficients
- Special Numbers
- Generating Functions

## 2. Random Variables

- Discrete Random Variables
- Continuous Random Variables
- Multivariate Distributions
- Moment Generating Functions

## 3. Conditional Probability

- Conditional Probability
- Conditional Expectation

## 4. Probability Bounds

- Markov Inequality
- Chebyshev Inequality
- Chernoff Bound
- Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality
- Jensen Inequality

## 5. Convergence of Random Variables

- Law of Large Numbers
- Central Limit Theorem

## 6. Statistical Inference

- Parametric Models
- Nonparametric Models
- Point Estimation
- Confidence Intervals
- Bootstrapping

## 7. Parametric Inference

- Maximum Likelihood Estimation

## 8. Bayesian Inference

- Maximum A Posteriori Estimation

## 9. Hypothesis Testing

- Permutation Tests
- Likelihood Ratio Tests
- Multiple Testing

## 10. Stochastic Processes

- [[Stochastic Process]]
- [[Markov Chain]] (Discrete-Time Processes)
- [[Classification of States in Markov Chains]]
- [[Stationary and Limiting Distributions in Markov Chains]]
- [[Chapman-Kolmogorov Equations]]
- [[Gambler's Ruin Formula]]
- Poisson Process
- Gaussian Process
- Continuous-Time Processes
- Birth-Death Process

## 11. Queuing Theory

- Exponential Models
- Open Queuing Networks
- Closed Queuing Networks
- Applications of Queuing Models
