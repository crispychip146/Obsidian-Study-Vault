# CSE 317 — Artificial Intelligence

> 3 hours/week · 3 credits

## Course Description

Artificial intelligence, intelligent agents, search, optimization,
adversarial search, constraint satisfaction, knowledge representation,
reasoning, planning, machine learning, reinforcement learning,
robotics, and AI ethics.
